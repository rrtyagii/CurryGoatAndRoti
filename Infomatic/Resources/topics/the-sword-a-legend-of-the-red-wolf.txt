The Sword: A Legend of the Red Wolf (Korean: 칼, 고두막한의 검) is an upcoming epic action adventure drama film directed by Kim Han-min starring Park Bo-gum. It revolves around a Goguryeo warrior, played by Park, and his quest to obtain a fabled sword called Godumakhan.

Premise
Set two years after the fall of Goguryeo's Pyongyang Castle, it centers around slave-turned-warrior Chil-seong (Park Bo-gum), also known as Red Wolf, who has lost his memory and gets caught up in a brutal conflict between factions over the sword of Godumakhan, a fabled sword in the kingdom of Goguryeo.

Production
Development
The film was first announced in October 2024 during the "Plus M X SLL Night" event held at the 29th Busan International Film Festival. It was selected by the Korea Creative Content Agency as the K-Content AI Pioneer Project for its incorporation of AI technology. It is produced by Big Stone Pictures, Blue Fire Studio, Red Ice Entertainment, and funded by Solaire Partners.

Casting
In March 2025, Park Bo-gum who had a role in director Kim Han-min's The Admiral: Roaring Currents (2014), was reported to lead Kim’s next film set in Goguryeo. Cha Seung-won joined the film two months later in May 2025 but exited the project in January 2026 due to scheduling conflicts. He was replaced by Vietnamese actor Dog Thành. Actor Joo Won and singer-actress Kim Hyeong-seo joined the cast in January 2026 ahead of filming.

Filming
Principal photography began in January 2026.

Release
The film is expected to premiere in the summer of 2027.
