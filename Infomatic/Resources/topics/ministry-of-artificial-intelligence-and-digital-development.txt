The Ministry of Artificial Intelligence and Digital Development of the Republic of Kazakhstan (MAIDD RK, Kazakh: Қазақстан Республикасының Жасанды интеллект және цифрлық даму министрлігі, romanized: Qazaqstan Respwblïkasınıñ Jasandı ïntellekt jäne cïfrlıq damw mïnïstrligi, ҚР ЖИЦДМ МИИЦР РК) is a central executive body of the Government of Kazakhstan, established in 2025.

History

On 18 September 2025, the President of the Republic of Kazakhstan issued a decree establishing the ministry.
