NLPearl is an Israeli artificial intelligence technology company specializing in conversational AI for voice-based customer interactions. Headquartered in Tel Aviv, the company develops AI agents, including AI voice agents and AI phone agents, designed to autonomously handle inbound and outbound telephone calls using natural language processing (NLP) technologies.

Its platform enables businesses to build, deploy, and manage automated phone agents capable of understanding spoken language, generating human-like responses, and executing a wide range of actions - including call routing, data collection, workflow automation, and integrations with external systems and APIs,  with minimal human intervention. NLPearl's technology is used across multiple industries for customer support, sales, and operational call automation.

History

NLPearl was established in 2022 by David Sztern, Ofek Darhi, Samuel Schwarcz, and Tal Alufi. The company focuses on applying natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning to automate voice communications traditionally handled by human contact centre agents. Over time, NLPearl expanded its platform to support AI-driven voice agents capable of managing inbound and outbound call workflows across multiple industries.

By 2024, NLPearl was selected as a finalist for the "T-Challenge," a global competition hosted by T-Mobile and Deutsche Telekom focused on the use of AI in telecommunications.

Products and technology

NLPearl's main offerings include Pearl Phone, Browser Pearl, and PearlVibe, AI-driven conversational agent models designed for voice-based automation.

PearlVibe is a model focused on the automated creation and configuration of AI agents. It enables businesses to generate conversational agents from high-level instructions by defining interaction flows, agent behaviors, and system integrations programmatically, reducing the need for manual setup. For example, the model can be used to configure a full AI-driven voice agent call center from a single prompt.

Pearl Phone and Browser Pearl integrate with existing telephony systems and customer relationship management (CRM) tools. These AI voice and phone agents are designed to manage routine phone interactions, support inbound and outbound call campaigns, and assist with tasks such as lead qualification, customer support, order inquiries, and appointment booking. The platform supports multilingual interactions and real-time analytics, with APIs that enable integration with CRM and workflow systems.
