"Papaoutai - Afro Soul" is a 2025 song by the two Swedish music producers "mikeeysmind" and "Chill77" that was released on December 20, 2025. It is an AI cover of the 2013 "Papaoutai" by Stromae.
In January, it was the highest new entry on the Global Spotify charts. In the week of January 24, 2026, it debuted at #2 on the Billboard World Digital Song Sales and #172 on the Billboard Global 200. Its corresponding TikTok sound plays in over 235,000 posts as of January 2026.

Reception
Zaghrah Anthony of Bona Magazine wrote that the remixed version "stays true to the original’s spirit but transforms it into a slower, more immersive production."
